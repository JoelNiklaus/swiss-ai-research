# Swiss AI research, startups and talent

Data behind the blog post ["Switzerland Does Top AI Research, but Too Little of It Stays"](https://huggingface.co/blog/joelniklaus/swiss-ai-research) by Joel Niklaus. Code: https://github.com/JoelNiklaus/swiss-ai-research. Each table is regenerated by the module named below (`uv run python -m swiss_ai.<module>`); the hand-collected tables list a source URL per row.

## Tables

| File | Module | Contents |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `papers.csv` | `papers` | Main-track papers 2010-2026 at CVPR, ICCV, ECCV, ACL, EMNLP, NAACL, NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR and AAAI from the DBLP dump (COLM and recent OpenReview years come from `openreview_papers.csv`). |
| `authorships.csv.gz` | `openalex` | One row per authorship of DBLP papers matched to OpenAlex, with countries after the Swiss raw-affiliation override. |
| `openreview_papers.csv`, `openreview_authorships.csv` | `openreview` | Accepted papers of ICLR 2022-2026, NeurIPS 2021-2025, ICML 2023-2026 and COLM 2024-2026, and author countries from OpenReview profile histories in the paper year. |
| `participation.csv` | `analysis` | Fractional and any-author country shares per venue and year. |
| `participation_per_capita.csv` | `analysis` | 2023-2025 papers per million residents (World Bank) and per 1,000 researchers (OECD MSTI). |
| `awards/` | `awards` | Hand-collected best and outstanding paper awards with Swiss institution and citizenship flags (citizenship checked only for authors with a Swiss link). |
| `award_ratios.csv` | `analysis` | Observed vs expected award papers per country, fractional and any-author, with Poisson intervals and robustness variants. |
| `citizen_winners.csv` | `analysis` | Swiss citizens among award winners against three population baselines. |
| `papercopilot_crosscheck.csv`, `papercopilot_countries.csv` | `crosscheck` | Pipeline country shares next to Paper Copilot's independent affiliation data for ICLR, ICML and NeurIPS 2023-2025. |
| `erc_by_country.csv` | `funding` | ERC Starting, Consolidator, Advanced and Synergy Grants by host country and call year from CORDIS. |
| `snsf_computer_science.csv` | `funding` | SNSF grants in information technology per year. |
| `investment.csv` | `investment` | AI Index 2026 private AI investment (Quid) and AI talent (Zeki) per country. |
| `vc_terms.csv` | `vc_terms` | Median pre-money valuation and round size by stage in Europe (including Switzerland) and the US in 2025, with the implied share of the company sold. |
| `citizenship_funnel.csv`, `doctoral_mobility.csv` | `citizenship` | BFS students and staff in the exact sciences by level and citizenship; OECD internationally mobile doctoral students. |
| `university_ventures_2025.csv`, `venture_kick_by_institution.csv` | `spinoffs` | ETH and EPFL 2025 venture figures and Venture Kick counts by institution. |
| `hand_collected/erc_swiss_nationals.csv` | hand-collected | Swiss-national vs Swiss-hosted ERC grantees per call, read from the ERC per-call statistics (2013-2020, 2025). |
| `hand_collected/erc_official_host_counts.csv`, `erc_2025_official_grantees.csv` | hand-collected | Official ERC host counts for Israel and Switzerland (2024-2026 calls) and the parsed 2025 results lists. |
| `hand_collected/professors_by_citizenship.csv` | hand-collected | Professors by citizenship at ETH Zurich, EPFL and all Swiss universities (BFS Basistabellen, 2010 and 2025). |
| `hand_collected/swiss_ai_exits.csv` | hand-collected | 49 acquisitions, asset deals and mergers of Swiss AI startups 2010-2026 with acquirer country; 9 rows are flagged borderline in `notes`. |
| `hand_collected/swiss_ai_startups.csv`, `swiss_ai_startup_founders.csv` | hand-collected | The 28 best-funded AI startups founded in Switzerland, their headquarters, and each founder's Swiss citizenship from the commercial register (SHAB/Zefix). |
| `hand_collected/vc_terms_europe_us.csv` | hand-collected | Chart data of Atomico's State of European Tech 2025 (Dealroom, to 30 September 2025): median valuations and round sizes by stage, Europe against the US. |
| `figures/` | `figures` | The blog post's figures as PNG (uploaded from `blog/figures/`). |
| `raw/ai_index/` | input | Unmodified AI Index 2026 chart data (CC BY-ND 4.0). |
