## ICCV

- **ICCV 1998 title.** Jeff Huang and the old PAMI TC page give "Maintaining Multiple Motion Model Hypotheses..." ACL 2015 corrections to your nlp.csv:

- Strubell et al. ("Learning Dynamic Feature Selection for Fast Sequential Prediction") was an **outstanding** paper, not best. ACL Wiki lists it as best paper, but the authors' own pages say "Outstanding Paper" and the proceedings Best Paper Session did not include it. Sources: strubell.github.io/publications, lvilnis.github.io, people.cs.umass.edu/~mccallum/pubs.html.
- The ACL 2015 Best Paper Session (https://aclanthology.org/P15-1000.pdf) contained Graham and AutoExtend (Rothe and Schütze). AutoExtend received the IBM Best Student Paper award (Rothe dissertation, https://edoc.ub.uni-muenchen.de/20938/7/Rothe_Sascha.pdf), so it is excluded. Graham is the only confirmed best paper.
- Sachan's outstanding award is stated in his bios, but no source ties it to "Learning Answer-Entailing Structures for Machine Comprehension". Keep it marked unverified.
- Yih et al. was added to gaps.csv as outstanding. ACL 2015 published no official outstanding list, so the list may still be incomplete.

These are the NAACL award labeling decisions, so they can be revisited:

- **2006.** ACL Wiki lists Haghighi and Klein as a best paper. Dan Klein's homepage (https://people.eecs.berkeley.edu/~klein/) and Haghighi's publication page call it Best Student Paper, so it is `other`.
- **2015.** The proceedings (https://aclanthology.org/N15-1000.pdf) mention "three best paper awards". ACL Wiki marks two of them (Krishnan and Eisenstein; Faruqui et al.) as student awards, so only Soricut and Och is `best`.
- **2016.** The best short paper (Klerke, Goldberg, Søgaard) is missing from ACL Wiki but listed in https://aclanthology.org/N16-1000.pdf. The four runners-up are `honorable_mention`.
- **2018.** All four papers were announced as outstanding papers, with "one will be named best paper" (https://aclanthology.org/N18-1000.pdf). ACL Wiki names ELMo, so ELMo is `best` and the other three are `outstanding`.
- **2022.** Every best paper award carried a category, and no award was uncategorized (https://2022.naacl.org/blog/best-papers/ and Anthology award tags). New method, new task (two tied) and efficient NLP are `best`. The human-centered special theme award is `other`. The Anthology tags the two outstanding papers as "honorable mention for contribution to methods/resources", but the blog calls them outstanding papers.
- **2024.** Columbia's news page calls "Teaching Language Models to Self-Improve" a Best Paper, but the official award slides list it as an outstanding paper.
- **2025.** SAC awards (9 papers) and the demo award are not listed in naacl.csv.
